## Reflection

For Project 4 I focused on translating the full system into visual communication for the Research-A-Thon audience. The most involved piece was the poster architecture diagram — placing four color-coded layer bands, nine nodes at absolute coordinates, and routing arrows cleanly in TikZ required multiple iterations to avoid overlapping labels. The Beamer presentation reused simplified versions of those diagrams for a 3-minute video format, which forced tighter choices about what to show versus narrate. The video was structured around a screen-recording demo of the live app, so the narration is intentionally loose enough to be delivered naturally over a live walkthrough.
